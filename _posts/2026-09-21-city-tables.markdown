A publication with old books and public theology still needs places to sit.

City Tables is for the morning counter, the corner table, the brewery window, the bookstore bench, the library room, the sidewalk conversation, and the small rituals that make a city feel less abstract.

Beulah and Main should notice these things because discovery is not only archival. It is also sensory and local: a cup sweating on a table, a chalkboard menu, a street turning gold, a friend arriving late, a book opened while the room gets louder.

This department can eventually hold guides, reviews, photo essays, interviews, and notes from cities worth walking.
